# Source photos — NOT published

This folder sits **outside** `public/`, so nothing here is copied into the
built site or reachable by URL. Keep anything sensitive here.

Everything inside `public/` is published verbatim at a guessable address even
if no page links to it. That is the reason these two files were moved out.

## uj-stores-3-ORIGINAL-UNBLURRED.jpg

The untouched original of the UJ Stores handover photo. The laptop screen shows
UJ Stores' real business figures — total sales, total purchases, sales
outstanding, net profit — plus customer and supplier names in the Recent Sales
and Recent Purchases tables.

The published version (`public/images/projects/uj-stores-3.jpg`) has that region
blurred. Keep this original only as a master copy; do not publish it.

## redzone-2-INVOICE-DO-NOT-PUBLISH.jpg

A real Red Zone sales invoice billed to **Tech World**, showing an outstanding
balance of Rs 11,137,050.

This names a third party and discloses their debt. Tech World has not agreed to
appear on the LapCircuit website, and Red Zone's permission alone would not
cover it. Do not publish this image.
